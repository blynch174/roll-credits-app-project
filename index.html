      Privacy

      Your festivals, watch history, and settings are saved on this device. If you make an account, they're also backed up with Google Firebase along with your email, so you can use them on other devices. Nobody else can see them, and nothing is sold or shared. Admins can see your email, when you last opened the app, and how many movies you've logged. Delete Account (tap your email above) erases the backup and the account. Export Backup gives you a copy any time.
